01 Research

A research agenda for combined judgment.

Most real decisions rest on many partial signals, each noisy on its own. Our work asks how to bring them together carefully: how to weigh them, how to say how sure we are, and how to make the result usable by people without a data team.

  1. Fig. 2

    Forecasting under uncertainty

    A single number hides what matters most: how wrong it might be. We build forecasts that report calibrated ranges, widening honestly as the horizon grows, so a plan can account for the plausible spread rather than one guess.

  2. Fig. 3

    Combining perspectives

    Different models and data sources see different parts of a problem. We study ensembles and reconciliation methods that weigh each view by how reliable it has been, and resolve them into one estimate that is better than any single source.

  3. Fig. 4

    Decision intelligence for small businesses

    Quantitative methods are usually built for teams with analysts. We work on making them usable by a shop owner with a spreadsheet: clear inputs, sensible defaults, and outputs phrased as decisions, such as how much to order and when.

  4. Fig. 5

    Spatial and market intelligence

    The value of a place depends on many signals at once: prices, neighborhood, demand and direction of travel. We study how to read property and location data side by side and summarize it into one clear view of what a place is worth.

03 About

About Anekone

Anekone is an AI research and product company. We work on a single question from several directions: how to combine many imperfect signals into one answer people can rely on. We publish what we learn and ship it as products that ordinary people and small businesses can use without specialist help.
